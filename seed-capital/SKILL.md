# Capital Strategy

Use for a financing schedule, capital gap, milestone budget or comparisons
of equity, convertible, grant, equipment or research financing. Start with
validated financial assumptions and explicit dates, milestones and terms.

Run [the workspace CLI](scripts/seed_workspace.py). Read
[the workflow](references/workflow.md) and the
[private workspace guide](references/workspace.md). Check the downside
minimum cash and reconcile funding inflow to planned disbursements.
Report missing timing and restrictions before presenting a recommendation.
